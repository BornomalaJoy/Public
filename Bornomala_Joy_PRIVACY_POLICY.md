# Privacy Policy

**App:** Bornomala  
**Developer:** BornomalaJoy  
**Effective date:** October 26, 2026

> **In short:** Bornomala is a learning app that helps young children learn the Bangla alphabet. We do not ask for accounts, emails, phone numbers, or locations. We do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking tools. Children’s learning progress stays on the device. The microphone is used only when a child taps the microphone button to practise saying a letter.

## 1. Who we are

Bornomala (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by BornomalaJoy, Dhaka, Bangladesh. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at [aninditarahaanu@gmail.com](mailto:aninditarahaanu@gmail.com).

## 2. Who the app is for

The app is designed for young children, and it is intended to be used together with, or under the supervision of, a parent or guardian. Because children use it, we have designed it to collect as little information as possible and to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable children’s privacy laws such as COPPA (United States) and the GDPR (European Union and United Kingdom), to the extent they apply.

## 3. Information we handle

**a) Child profiles (stored only on the device).** The app lets several children share one device by creating local profiles. A profile consists of a name or nickname and a picture chosen from the avatars built into the app. We recommend using a nickname instead of a full real name. Profiles are never sent to us or to any server. We do not offer accounts or logins.

**b) Learning progress (stored only on the device).** The app saves which letters a child has practised, tracing progress, and quiz results, linked to the child’s local profile. This data stays on the device and is not uploaded, synced, or shared.

**c) Microphone and speech practice.** When a child taps the microphone button, the app asks for microphone permission (you can allow or deny it) and listens for a few seconds so it can check whether the child said the letter or word correctly. The app does not record, save, or send audio to us. To convert speech to text, the app uses the speech recognition service built into the Android device, which is provided by Google. Depending on the device and its settings, that service may process the audio on the device or send it to Google’s servers; this processing is governed by [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not by us. The app only receives the recognised text, compares it with the expected letter or word, and then discards it. If microphone permission is denied, the rest of the app continues to work.

**d) Learning content downloaded from the internet.** Letter sounds, pictures, and tracing guides are downloaded from a content library hosted on GitHub and delivered through the jsDelivr content delivery network, then stored on the device so the app can work offline. As with any internet request, these services can see technical information such as the device’s IP address and browser/app type when content is requested. We do not send them any child profile, progress, or identifier, and we do not receive this technical information ourselves. See the privacy policies of [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net) for how they handle it.

**e) What we do not collect.** We do not collect real names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, photos, videos, contacts, precise or approximate location, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers such as IMEI, MAC address, or SIM serial number.

## 4. Ads, analytics, and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no behavioural tracking, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools that collect personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

## 5. Permissions the app requests

- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** only for the speech-practice and speaking-check features, used only after a child taps the microphone button.
- **Internet access:** only to download learning content (see 3d).

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, photos, or storage permissions.

## 6. Parental gate

Settings, deleting a profile, and any link that leaves the app are protected by a simple parental gate (for example, a short maths question), so that young children do not reach them by accident.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Because profiles and progress live only on the device, you are in control of them. A parent or guardian can delete an individual profile (after passing the parental gate), which also deletes that child’s progress and quiz results from the device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app removes all data the app has stored. We hold no copy of this data, so we cannot recover it for you.

## 8. Parents’ and guardians’ rights

Since we do not collect or hold personal information about children, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete. If you believe we have received personal information about a child, or you have any privacy request, contact us at [aninditarahaanu@gmail.com](mailto:aninditarahaanu@gmail.com) and we will respond promptly and delete anything we find. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under local data protection law, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Security

Data kept on the device is protected by the Android operating system’s app sandbox. Learning content is downloaded over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but collecting almost nothing greatly reduces the risk.

## 10. Third-party services

- **Android speech recognition (Google):** converts a child’s spoken answer to text (see 3c).
- **GitHub and jsDelivr:** host and deliver learning content (see 3d).

We do not allow any other third party to collect data through the app. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before releasing that version.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If a change affects how children’s information is handled, we will make it clearly visible in the app and in the store listing before it takes effect.
